Living roadmap · updated 24 September 2026

English Memory Trainer

A deterministic Reading and Writing learning engine with replaceable tutors. The A1–C2 curriculum, the Phase 2 vertical slice, the Phase 3 automaticity layer (memorized frames, drill blocks, a short relearning ladder) and the Phase 4 lean brief → report protocol are all complete. The tutor runs each lesson in chat and rules on every item; the engine only proposes a brief, records the report, scores and schedules.

Eighteen open questions, all non-blocking

56 questions are already resolved. The open ones split into four groups:

  • OPEN-33–36: four trigger-based v2 extension candidates (exam profile, bulk exercise packs, active-policy manifest, external benchmark ingress).
  • OPEN-37/38: automaticity-layer calibration — threshold tuning and the article-tier ceiling.
  • OPEN-39: placement-form coverage and rubric hand-off for the real placement forms.
  • OPEN-40–43: findings from the 2026-09-23 live placement run; OPEN-43 is narrowed to placement's objective-check paths only — ordinary lessons keep the tutor, not an answer key, as the judge.
