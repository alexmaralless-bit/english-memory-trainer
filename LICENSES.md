# Licensing at a glance

This repository is licensed in two parts, and the split is not cosmetic.

## Software — MIT

`src/`, `tests/`, `tools/`, `pyproject.toml`, and the development knowledge
base (`wiki/`, `staging/`, `docs/`, `agent-skills/`, `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`,
`README.md`) are covered by the MIT licence in `LICENSE`.

## Curriculum data — CC BY-SA 4.0

Everything under `curriculum/` is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0; see
`curriculum/LICENSE`.

Parts of that data are derived from sources published under CC BY-SA 4.0 —
wordfreq, the New General Service List and the Business Service List — and
ShareAlike applies on distribution. Whether the selected lexicon constitutes
Adapted Material was deliberately not settled: CC BY-SA 4.0 is applied to the
whole directory, which satisfies the obligation either way.

Per-source attribution, the notices and the exact nature of the changes are
recorded in `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`. Raw upstream datasets are not redistributed;
`curriculum/lexicon/_provenance.yaml` records versions and checksums.
