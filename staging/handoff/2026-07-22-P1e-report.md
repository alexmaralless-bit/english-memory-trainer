## TOEFL coverage

The TOEFL track is text-only and contains no Listening or Speaking topic:

| Level | Count | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| B1 | 8 | skimming, scanning, paraphrase matching, vocabulary in context, paragraph organization, sentence building, academic email, claim + reason discussion response |
| B2 | 13 | academic main idea and evidence, inference, rhetorical purpose, text organization, contextual word completion, source notes, integrated synthesis, thesis, argument development, cohesion, academic register, discussion response |
| C1 | 5 | dense passage synthesis, implicit qualification, source integration, nuanced counterargument, timed response control |

The requested reading-to-notes-to-synthesis chain is explicit:

```text
toefl-reading.scanning-key-detail
  -> toefl-writing.source-note-taking
  -> toefl-writing.integrated-synthesis
  -> toefl-writing.source-integration
```

Independent/academic argument develops through:

```text
toefl-writing.discussion-claim-reason
  -> toefl-writing.thesis-position
  -> toefl-writing.argument-development
  -> toefl-writing.counterargument-nuance
  -> toefl-writing.timed-response-control
```

No C2 TOEFL-specific topic was added. The stated target is TOEFL B2-C1; C2 instead supplies transferable
editorial precision, dense reading, source-voice balance, and argument architecture without overfitting the
program to another exam tier.

## Density decisions

### B1

B1 is the nearest learning zone and therefore continues every topic-bearing track. Density emphasizes
connected explanations, production of the grammar tail, project communication, independent everyday
tasks, and the first eight TOEFL skills. Existing word-formation topics remain intact.

### B2

B2 is the densest new level because it is the lower target band for TOEFL. It carries full production of
the advanced grammar tail, technical argument, multi-source synthesis, professional negotiation, critical
reading, and thirteen TOEFL-specific topics.

### C1

C1 shifts from breadth to precision: critical reading, evidence-qualified synthesis, decision memos,
cohesion, stance, register, and governance communication dominate. Everyday Life and Online & Informal
continue at lower density so advanced competence does not become work-only or artificially formal.

### C2

C2 is deliberately compact. Its role is editorial and interpretive control: ambiguity, interdisciplinary
synthesis, complex argument architecture, high-stakes diplomacy, model-risk communication, and
audience-specific voice. Repeating broad everyday scenarios here would add volume without a distinct C2
can-do result.

### Word formation and lexicon

Word Formation continues sparsely at every level because patterns remain useful for academic reading, but
P.1e does not invent the B1-C2 examples needed to teach them. All lexical references are empty until the
pinned NAWL and authored TOEFL lexicon pass in P.4e.

## Canon/task contradictions found

### 1. `weak` in the task vs `soft` in canon and validator

The task requests `advisory_prerequisites` with `strong`/`weak`. The accepted curriculum contract and
`src/english_trainer/curriculum/validate.py` use `strong`/`soft`; the validator traverses only those two
keys. Using `weak` would silently remove those edges from dangling-reference, level-inversion, and cycle
checks. P.1e therefore uses `strong`/`soft` throughout.

Suggested owner correction: change `weak` to `soft` in the P.1e task text. No canon patch is required.

### 2. Expected body warnings are suppressed when dimensions exist

The task correctly requires `dimensions` on skeleton topics and expects `body not yet authored` warnings
for B1-C2. The current validator warns only when **both** `dimensions` and `mastery_criteria` are absent.
Consequently the 120 new skeleton topics do not warn, although they intentionally lack mastery criteria;
only the two pre-existing B1 topics (which lack both fields) warn.

Suggested future patch: for unauthored levels, warn whenever `mastery_criteria` is absent, independently of
whether skeleton dimensions have already been selected. This is a validator/reporting issue, not a P.1e
content error; `src/` and `tests/` were read-only for this task.
