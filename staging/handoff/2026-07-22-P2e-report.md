## Mastery and explanation decisions

`ru-allowed` remains available at every level. The exercises and examples are English, but contrastive
Russian explanations remain useful for article, word-order, aspect/tense, false-friend, and calque traps;
removing that support solely because a topic is C1 or C2 would not demonstrate English mastery.

Thresholds rise with the precision and transfer demands of the level:

| Level | Recognition | Controlled / spontaneous production | Transfer | Independent attempts | Retention |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| B1 | 70 | 75 | 75 | 2 | 30 days / 2 confirmations |
| B2 | 75 | 78 | 80 | 2 | 45 days / 2 confirmations |
| C1 | 80 | 82 | 85 | 3 | 60 days / 3 confirmations |
| C2 | 85 | 88 | 90 | 3 | 90 days / 3 confirmations |

Only dimensions declared by the accepted skeleton receive criteria. C1-C2 require more independent
evidence and longer retention because their can-do outcomes depend on precision under new audiences,
sources, and rhetorical conditions, not merely recognition of a form.

## Repository contradictions and owner follow-up

No contradiction with the accepted wiki canon was found. The two follow-ups below are repository
data/test mismatches, not proposals to change the learning contract.

### 1. Two accepted B1 sketches have no `dimensions`

`word-formation.surpass-out` and `word-formation.quality-suffix` predate P.1e and have no skeleton
`dimensions` field. P.2e was forbidden to modify skeleton fields, so the omission is preserved. Their
bodies use the only dimensions supported by their can-do statements and the advanced word-formation
policy: `recognition` and `transfer`.

When the owner adds B1-C2 to `AUTHORED_LEVELS`, both topics will fail the strict authored-level check
until the owner applies this skeleton repair to each topic:

```yaml
dimensions: [recognition, transfer]
```

No wiki change is required; this is a defect in the two pre-existing skeleton records.

### 2. A read-only test requires unfinished B1 content

`tests/curriculum/test_program.py::test_real_program_validates_clean` currently asserts that at least one
`body not yet authored` warning exists and comments that the B1 skeleton is intentionally unauthored.
That assertion is incompatible with the P.2e acceptance condition that the final warning count be zero.
Tests were read-only for this task, so it was not changed. Owner follow-up after acceptance:

```python
# Old
assert any("not yet authored" in warning for warning in report.warnings)

# New
assert report.warnings == []
```

### 3. Unrelated in-progress memory CLI work breaks one registry assertion

The working tree already contained uncommitted `memory.render`, `memory.rebuild`, and `memory.check`
registry work outside P.2e. `tests/cli/test_cli.py::test_registry_matches_published_surface` has not yet
been updated for those commands. P.2e did not touch, stage, or commit any of those files.
